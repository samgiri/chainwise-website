Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you submit through The Chainwise's email/SMS alert signup forms — on the homepage, the risk dashboard, the research section, and the footer.

What we collect

  • Email address — required to sign up for risk alerts or case-study updates.
  • Mobile number — optional, only collected if you explicitly opt in to SMS alerts.
  • We do not require an account and do not collect payment information through these forms.

How we use it

  • To send you alerts about newly analyzed high-risk protocols.
  • To notify you when new case studies or research are published.
  • We do not sell your email or phone number to third parties.

How it's stored today

Submissions are validated and logged by our backend. We have not yet connected a dedicated email or SMS delivery provider — so signing up captures your interest, but alert delivery is not live yet. We'll update this policy when that changes.

Your choices

  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time by contacting us (see below).
  • Every alert email we eventually send will include an unsubscribe option.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to privacy@thechainwise.io.

This is a plain-language starter policy describing our current, minimal data handling. It has not been reviewed by legal counsel and does not make specific claims about regulatory compliance (e.g. GDPR, CCPA). Treat it as a description of practice, not a legal guarantee.
