What the risk score means

0–33 · Low Risk

Few or no warning signs found in what we could check.

Action: Still do your own research — a low score isn't a green light, it means we found less to worry about.

34–66 · Medium Risk

Some warning signs found — things worth understanding before you commit money.

Action: Read the findings carefully. Consider starting small if you proceed.

67–89 · High Risk

Several serious warning signs found.

Action: Strong caution. Most people should avoid unless you personally verify the findings.

90–100 · Critical Risk

Severe warning signs — patterns commonly seen in scams or collapses.

Action: Avoid. Don't invest based on this score alone — but also don't invest despite it without independent verification.

No score · Insufficient evidence

We couldn't gather enough information to assess this.

Action: Never treat "no data" as "safe" — it means we don't know, not that it's fine.

A lower score means fewer risk indicators were detected in what we could check — it does not guarantee the contract is safe.
