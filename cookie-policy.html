This policy explains how The Chainwise uses cookies and similar browser technologies when you visit our website.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. They can support essential site functions, remember preferences, and help site operators understand general usage.

How we use them

The Chainwise may use strictly necessary cookies or similar local storage to provide core website functions, maintain security, and remember choices you make. We may also use limited analytics technologies to understand aggregate site performance and improve the experience.

Third-party services

Some website resources or services may be provided by third parties, such as hosting, fonts, or analytics providers. Those providers may process technical information according to their own privacy and cookie policies.

Your choices

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary storage may prevent parts of the site from working as intended. If optional cookies are introduced, we will provide an appropriate choice before using them where required.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site and applicable requirements change. The date at the top of this page shows the latest revision.

Questions
