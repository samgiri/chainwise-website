About The Chainwise

The Chainwise is a DeFi risk intelligence platform built to flag high-risk smart contracts, treasury anomalies, and structural red flags before capital is exposed.

Our analysis runs on a 6-dimension risk engine covering contract verification, admin controls, upgradeability, token restrictions, ownership concentration, and governance — plus 2 roadmap dimensions (liquidity and exploit/anomaly signals) not yet built — aimed at evidence-based, on-chain-verifiable findings rather than subjective opinions.

The Chainwise is built by Satoshi Lab.
