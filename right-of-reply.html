Right of Reply

Our case studies and risk assessments name specific protocols and describe on-chain behavior we believe indicates elevated risk. We want those findings to be accurate and fair.

If you represent a project discussed in one of our published assessments and believe a specific factual claim is incorrect or missing important context, email contact@thechainwise.io with the claim in question and supporting evidence. We will review submissions and update or annotate published findings where a correction is warranted.

This is a factual-correction channel, not a takedown mechanism — our risk assessments are opinions formed from on-chain evidence, and we won't remove a finding solely because a project disagrees with our conclusions.
